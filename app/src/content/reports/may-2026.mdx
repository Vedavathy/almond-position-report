## Sales and Commitments

New sales registered in May reached 124.94 million lbs., up a notable 21.5% from the 102.80 million lbs. recorded in May of last year. That acceleration in buying activity is one of the more consequential data points in this report. Whatever the combination of factors, the direction is clear and constructive for the market.

Together, these figures paint a picture of a market where more of the available supply has been spoken for relative to this time last crop year, giving handlers and growers a somewhat firmer footing as the season winds toward its close.
